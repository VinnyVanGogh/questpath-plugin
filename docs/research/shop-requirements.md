## 1. Executive Summary & Root-Cause Findings

### 1.1 Why Quest Route Sent the Player to the Warriors' Guild (`RUNELITE-212`)
In `quests.json`, **13 shops** carry `"reachability": {"unknown": true}` emitted by the upstream Go exporter (`questpath/internal/export/questgraph.go:265`) whenever a shop or location gate could not be reduced to a single quest ID or a single `{{SCP|Skill|Level}}` token. The Go exporter's documented contract states:
> *"Unknown is true when a shop or location gate could not be resolved to quest IDs (or skill levels). A plugin must never read a shop with Unknown=true as tradeable."*

However, in `questpath-plugin`:
1. **`QuestGraph.Reachability`** (`src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/questpath/QuestGraph.java:540-578`) only deserializes `required_skills`, `required_quest_ids`, and `required_quest_ids_started_only`. **It is missing `@SerializedName("unknown") private boolean unknown;`.**
2. **`ShopReachability.of()`** (`src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/questpath/ShopReachability.java:96-165`) inspects `requiredSkills()` and `requiredQuestIds()`, sees two empty lists for any `{"unknown": true}` shop, and returns **`Verdict.OPEN`**.
3. As a result, all **13 `"unknown": true` shops**—including **`Warrior Guild Armoury`** (where `Bronze med helm` is stocked at 5 vs. 3 at `Horvik's Armour Shop.`), **`Warrior Guild Food Shop`**, **`Myths' Guild Herbalist`**, **`Baba Yaga's Magic Shop.`**, and the 4 **`Darkmeyer`** shops—are treated as completely unrestricted and outrank open shops whenever their stock is higher (`ShopRouteStops.compareCandidates()` sorts by `stockFor` descending first).

### 1.2 Summary of All Shop & Source Gaps in `quests.json` v12
1. **13 shops with `"reachability": {"unknown": true}` treated as `OPEN`:** Require deserializing `unknown` in `QuestGraph.Reachability` (fail-closed as `LOCKED`) AND replacing `"unknown": true` with structured compound/quest/skill requirements (Warriors' Guild 130 Attack+Strength or 99 in either; Myths' Guild `Dragon Slayer II`; Lunar Isle `Lunar Diplomacy`; Darkmeyer `Sins of the Father`; Gu'Tanoth `Watchtower`; Varrock Rat Pit `Ratcatchers`; Seb's Shipyard `42 Sailing` + `Pandemonium`).
